This mod recreates the Manager of La Manchaland Don Quixote's weapon from Limbus Company in Terraria. 

[img]https://i.ibb.co/Gy7pChZ/slm-newooapreview.webp[/img]


Craft "La Sangre" with a Broken Hero's Sword and Bloody Tear at a Mythril Anvil. When it is in your inventory, all melee damage is converted into "Bloodfeast," which can be spent to greatly enhance the weapon...

This mod is a work in progress. Upcoming features include:
[list]
[*] [strike]Animation for enhancing La Sangre [/strike]
[*] Configurable Bloodfeast resource bar
[*] Improved VFX
[/list]
 
SFX are from Limbus Company and will be removed upon request.
